About Me

A bit more detail on my background, skills, and how I like to work.

My name is Palak Palak. I’m a Full-Stack Developer in training and a software development student at NBCC Moncton in the Programmer Analyst stream. I enjoy learning by building real applications and experimenting with different parts of the stack—from the database and APIs up to the user interface.

Recently, I’ve worked on projects such as an invoice generator in Node.js, a library system in Spring Boot, and a recipe/approval application using ASP.NET MVC. These projects helped me practice routing, data modeling, authentication, validation, and user-friendly UIs.

I’m motivated by clear requirements, well-structured codebases, and opportunities to collaborate with others. I like breaking down tasks, asking questions when needed, and improving my work based on feedback.
